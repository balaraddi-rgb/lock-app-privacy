Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 14, 2026

Plain-language summary: Lock does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no servers, no analytics, no accounts, and no network access. The app's sole function is to lock your device screen when you tap its icon.

About Lock

Lock is a minimal Android utility that locks your device screen with a single tap. It uses the Android Accessibility Service solely to perform the screen-lock action — equivalent to pressing the power button — without any background activity, data access, or network communication.

Data We Do Not Collect

Lock collects absolutely no data of any kind. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Device identifiers or hardware information
  • Location data
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • Screen content or accessibility event data
  • Contacts, messages, or any on-device content
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data

Accessibility Service Usage

Lock requests the Android Accessibility Service permission for one purpose only: to call GLOBAL_ACTION_LOCK_SCREEN, which locks the device screen. The Accessibility Service in Lock:

  • Does not read or monitor screen content
  • Does not observe or record accessibility events
  • Does not interact with other applications
  • Performs no background activity
  • Is only invoked when you explicitly tap the Lock icon

Network Access

Lock does not request internet permission and makes no network connections of any kind. No data is transmitted to any server, third party, or remote service.

Third-Party Services

Lock does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or crash reporting services.

Data Retention

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to retain or delete.

Children's Privacy

Lock does not collect data from any users, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for use by all ages.
